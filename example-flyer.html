[Your Organization Name]

Text your community PIN to
1-844-321-ALRT (2578)

📢 Community Updates

How to customize this flyer for your community:

  1. 1 Replace "[Your Organization Name]" with your organization (e.g., "Coke County Emergency Management")
  2. 2 Replace "1234" with your community's assigned PIN
  3. 3 Print and post at community centers, fire stations, libraries, and public buildings
  4. 4 Share digitally on your website, social media, and community newsletters
